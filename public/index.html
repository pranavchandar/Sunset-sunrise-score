How reliable is this?
  • Forecast models give cloud cover as a percentage over a grid several km wide. They can't see texture, and broken altocumulus versus a flat sheet changes everything.
  • Low-cloud forecasts in particular are often wrong.
  • Reliable for today and tomorrow; weak beyond 48 hours.
  • Expect roughly a 70% hit rate. Treat the score as a probability-like guide, not a promise.
